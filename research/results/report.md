# Ece Kirli in İzmir: Evidence Assessment

## Finding

**The supplied sources do not establish who Ece Kirli is, whether a person by that name is associated with İzmir, or whether any of the people named in the sources are the person sought.** A biographical report would therefore require unsupported identification and should not be presented as fact.

The İzmir article describes the city, not Ece Kirli. The İzmir Institute of Technology (İYTE) architecture staff excerpts name **Ece Güleç**, whose surname differs from Kirli; they provide no basis for treating the two names as referring to the same person. The supplied YouTube excerpt names **Aleyna Yorulmaz**, not Ece Kirli. ## Verified facts and unresolved questions

The supplied material verifies that İzmir is a city in Turkey and that the cited İYTE staff page lists a person named Ece Güleç. **Neither fact verifies the identity, occupation, education, accomplishments, or İzmir connection of Ece Kirli.** In particular, a shared first name and an institutional page associated with the İzmir area are not sufficient to match two people with different surnames. ([Wikipedia contributors, n.d.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0zmir); [İYTE Department of Architecture, n.d.](https://architecture.iyte.edu.tr/akademik-personel))

The supplied excerpts are not evidence that *no* public information about Ece Kirli exists. They support only the narrower finding that **these sources provide no reliable, person-specific information about her**. The most defensible conclusion is thus to leave her identity and connection to İzmir **unverified**, rather than attach another person’s academic profile to her or expand city background into a purported biography. A longer report claiming “every information” about her would add speculation, not substantiated detail.
